# Implementation sequence

1. **Project foundation, data dependencies, shared contracts, and health service — current.** This plan establishes the pinned Go toolchain, PostgreSQL/Redis/NATS development stack, reversible schema baseline, generated Protobuf/OpenAPI/sqlc contracts, health and loopback metrics endpoints, deterministic verification, and the end-to-end smoke test. Its independent exit condition is a clean regeneration diff, passing pinned lint/vet/race/build checks and integration tests, and a smoke run that proves PostgreSQL readiness failure and recovery while liveness stays healthy. Redis 8.8.1 remains on the recorded unmodified AGPLv3 local-development path; production use is blocked until written legal approval or a recorded commercial license exists.

2. **Device registration, signed/HPKE configuration, candidate scheduling, and distribution.** This plan adds versioned device enrollment and encrypted, signed, allowlisted candidate configuration delivery without exposing raw tunnel-core configuration. Its independent exit condition is a contract-driven integration test in which an enrolled device authenticates, obtains a decryptable configuration bound to that device and version, rejects tampering/replay/unknown fields, and continues from its last known valid configuration when the control plane is unavailable.

3. **Node agent, POP desired state, health aggregation, and capacity scheduling.** This plan adds the node-agent boundary, reconciled POP state, privacy-bounded health signals, and capacity-aware candidate selection. Its independent exit condition is a multi-POP test that removes an unhealthy or full node from new assignments within the bounded reconciliation interval, retains healthy capacity, and emits no device, destination, address, credential, or raw-error labels or logs.

4. **Tunnel Engine state machine, automatic/manual switching, and four-platform adapters.** This plan implements the stable Tunnel Engine contract above sing-box plus native Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS lifecycle adapters, with Xray and sing-box kept as separate operating-system processes and release batches outside the proprietary control-plane binary. Its independent exit condition is the same conformance suite passing on all four platform adapters for cold start, last-known configuration, automatic failover, manual switching, cancellation, and process cleanup. Distribution remains blocked on documented sing-box GPLv3+ complete-corresponding-source/store review and Xray-core MPL-2.0 covered-file/source-notice review.

5. **Accounts, devices, entitlements, traffic ledger, quota leases, and hard stop.** This plan adds authoritative account/device/entitlement state, idempotent usage accounting, bounded quota leases, and server-enforced hard stops across protocols. Its independent exit condition is a fault-injection test proving duplicate and reordered usage events do not change the ledger total, expired or exhausted leases stop access, and the same test traffic is metered consistently across Xray and sing-box paths within the accepted tolerance.

6. **Catalog, Apple/Google/Web/crypto payments, and refunds.** This plan adds the product catalog and idempotent purchase, renewal, cancellation, chargeback, and refund lifecycles for each payment channel without granting entitlement from an unverified client claim. Its independent exit condition is a provider-sandbox contract suite proving signed event verification, replay-safe ledger transitions, entitlement convergence after out-of-order events, and complete refund or revocation handling for every supported channel.

7. **Referral rebates, affiliate commissions, and rewarded-ad rewards.** This plan adds abuse-resistant attribution and immutable reward accounting while isolating advertising from VPN traffic and tunnel processes. Its independent exit condition is a deterministic ledger test proving self-referral, replay, duplicate conversion, and conflicting attribution are rejected, valid rewards settle once, and ad unavailability or consent refusal never prevents VPN, account, payment, or invitation flows.

8. **Four-platform commercial UI, five-language localization, RTL, release, and store compliance.** This plan delivers native Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS product surfaces in Simplified Chinese, English, Russian, Persian, and Japanese, including RTL behavior, accessibility, packaging, upgrade/rollback, privacy disclosures, and store materials. Its independent exit condition is a signed release-candidate matrix passing native UI, localization/RTL, accessibility, installer/upgrade, privacy, and store-submission checks on all four platforms, with the Redis production license gate and the separate sing-box/Xray process, source, notice, modification, and application-store legal gates recorded as approved rather than bypassed.
